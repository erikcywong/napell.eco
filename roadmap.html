Roadmap路线图

Gates, not promises. 用闸门，不用口号。

Each phase unlocks only when the previous one earns it. Expansion before proof is how franchise systems die — we simply refuse to run that experiment on our partners' money. 上一阶段不达标，下一阶段不开启。在证明之前扩张，是加盟体系死亡的标准姿势——我们拒绝拿加盟商的钱做这个实验。

PHASE 1 · 0→1 · ~30 MONTHS · PROVE THE MODEL阶段一 · 0→1 · 约 30 个月 · 证明模型

Build the sample. Recruit nobody.做样板，不招加盟。

  • 1 fully-owned demo warehouse (6,000 plants, ¥2.0M)1 座全资示范仓（6,000 株，¥200 万）
  • 3–5 roasters in joint cupping + small-lot offtake3–5 家烘焙商联合杯测 + 小批量包销
  • Third-party residue / food-safety / cupping certification第三方农残/食品安全/杯测认证
  • Field-validate every assumption in the financial model用实测数据校验财务模型的每一条假设
GATE → PHASE 2:闸门 → 阶段二： 3 consecutive batches ≥84 cupping · ≥2 roasters repurchasing · actual unit cost within 15% of model. 连续 3 批次杯测 ≥84 · ≥2 家烘焙商复购 · 实测成本与模型偏差 <15%。
PHASE 2 · 1→10 · JOINT-OPERATION NETWORK阶段二 · 1→10 · 联营网络

Certified joint-operation warehouses, 3–5 regions联营认证仓，3–5 个区域

  • Regional partners sign floor-price / first-purchase buyback agreements区域合伙方签署保底/优先回购协议
  • Early partner warehouses double as regional propagation nodes (12,000 seedlings/yr each)早期联营仓兼任区域繁育节点（各 12,000 株/年）
  • QC platform live: telemetry, traceability, benchmarking across warehouses品控平台上线：遥测、追溯、跨仓基准
  • Prove partner-level payback before any talk of "franchise"跑通加盟商回本模型之前，不谈"加盟"二字
GATE → PHASE 3:闸门 → 阶段三： ≥3 partner warehouses at steady state · first partners expanding on their own capital · buyback fulfilled at 100% for 4 consecutive quarters. ≥3 座联营仓进入稳态 · 首批加盟商自掏腰包扩仓 · 回购履约连续 4 个季度 100%。
PHASE 3 · 10→100 · PLATFORM阶段三 · 10→100 · 平台化

Open the license. Keep the crown jewels.开放授权，守住王冠。

  • Standardized modules + certification body + data platform as products标准模块、认证体系、数据平台产品化
  • Regional hubs + urban near-field supply to captive roasters & hotels区域中心仓 + 城市近场直供自有烘焙渠道
  • Technology licensing internationally, with local compliance partners技术授权出海，绑定本地合规伙伴
  • Nutrients, sensors and data stay platform-side — forever营养液、传感器与数据永远在平台手里

The one-sentence strategy:一句话战略： Open the warehouse cooperation and certification system; guard the formulation, the QC standard, the buyback channel and the brand. Partners aren't buying equipment — they're buying the certainty of making money. Let the first ten partners get rich, and the flywheel turns itself. 开放种植仓合作与认证体系，守住配方、品控标准、回购通道与品牌。加盟商买的不是设备，是赚钱的确定性。先让前十位加盟商赚到钱，飞轮自己会转。
