NAPELL Ecosystem Platform · CEO Letter

Sell Certainty,
Not Warehouses. 卖的是确定性，
不是仓。

Over the past five years we proved aeroponic coffee can be grown. The next five years are about proving it can be replicated — certified, financed, and scaled. And the people who replicate it must profit first. 过去五年，我们证明了雾培咖啡种得出来。未来五年，要证明的是它能被复制——被认证、被融资、被规模化。而复制它的人，必须先赚到钱。

We are not opening a franchise. We are opening a certified production network — technology licensing + certified growing warehouses + floor-price buyback + a data & QC platform. What partners buy is not a warehouse; it is a repeatable, bankable output capability. 我们开放的不是加盟，而是一张认证生产网络——技术授权 + 认证种植仓 + 保底回购 + 数据品控平台。加盟商买到的不是一套设备，而是一种可重复、可入账的稳定出品能力。

The Flywheel飞轮

The first tooth is an order, not a franchisee. 飞轮的第一齿是订单，不是加盟商。

Recruiting partners before real purchase orders exist means asking them to carry our cold-start risk. That model collapses. So the flywheel starts at the demo warehouse. 没有真实采购订单就去招加盟，等于让加盟商替我们承担冷启动风险——模式必然崩。所以飞轮从示范仓开始转。

WE OPEN我们开放

The network, not the recipe开放网络，不开放配方

Certified-warehouse cooperation, joint ventures, licensing with audit rights, and our buyback channel. Partners get everything needed to produce within spec. 认证仓合作、联营、带审计权的技术授权，以及回购通道。加盟商拿到的是"规格内生产"所需的全部。

WE KEEP我们守住

Four things never leave the building四样东西永不出门

Nutrient formulations · environment-control algorithms · root-zone microbiology · the data platform. Key control units ship as black boxes; consumables are supplied by us. 营养液配方 · 环控算法 · 根系菌群管理 · 数据平台。关键控制单元以黑箱交付，耗材由平台统一供应。

Revenue truth:收入真相： green-bean buyback is only ~33% of a warehouse's steady-state revenue; seedling propagation is ~62%. Every certified warehouse doubles as a regional propagation node — the network grows its own growth. 生豆回购只占单仓稳态收入约 33%，种苗繁育占约 62%。每座认证仓同时是区域繁育节点——这张网络自己长出自己的扩张燃料。
