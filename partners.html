Partnering合作

Light franchise. Heavy operations. Hard buyback. 轻加盟，重运营，强回购。

Partners bring land, capital and local operations. We bring genetics, inputs, algorithms, QC and the sales channel. Neither succeeds without the other. 加盟商出场地、资金与本地运营；我们出种苗、投入品、算法、品控与销售通道。谁也离不开谁，这才是绑定的正确姿势。

Quality: six unifications品控：六个统一

Coffee is not a phone. Ten warehouses with ten flavor profiles means roasters walk. Consistency is the product.咖啡不是手机。十个仓十个风味，烘焙商扭头就走。一致性本身就是产品。

① Cultivar & seedlings① 统一品种种苗

Platform-propagated, trace-coded clones only.仅限平台繁育、带追溯码的克隆苗。

② Nutrients & climate② 统一营养与环控

One formulation, one control curve, pushed remotely.同一配方、同一条控制曲线，远程下发。

③ Harvest & processing③ 统一采收后处理

Same picking standards, same wash protocol, same drying curve.同一采摘标准、同一水洗规程、同一干燥曲线。

④ Batch traceability④ 批次追溯

Every bag carries a code back to warehouse, week and sensor log.每袋生豆可回溯到仓、周次与传感器日志。

⑤ Third-party verification⑤ 第三方验证

Residue, food-safety and cupping tests by accredited labs, annually.资质实验室年检：农残、食品安全、杯测。

⑥ Tiered buyback⑥ 分级收购

Specialty / commercial / downgraded — never a one-price blanket.精品/商业/降级三级——绝不一刀切价。

IP: open, but never naked知识产权：开放，但不裸奔

Black-box the core核心黑箱化

Nutrients, algorithms and root-zone microbiology ship as sealed control units. Warehouse modules are deliberately incomplete without them.配方、算法与菌群以封闭控制单元交付。没有黑箱，仓体模块有意"不完整"。

Contract as circuit breaker合同即断路器

Scope-of-use, territorial exclusivity, no reverse engineering, non-compete, and consumables single-sourcing — breach kills the license, not just a clause.使用范围、区域排他、禁止反向工程、竞业限制、耗材单一来源——违约终止的是整个授权，不是某一条款。

Six contract pillars协议六大支柱

1 · Territory1 · 区域保护

Exclusion radius per warehouse — we don't cannibalize our own partners.单仓排他半径——我们不做自己人的内耗。

2 · Output & quality covenants2 · 产能品质对赌

Consecutive failing batches trigger remediation, then exit.连续不达标触发整改，再不达标触发退出。

3 · Buyback standard, written hard3 · 回购标准写死

Cupping score, moisture, defect count, residue, delivery window — all numeric, all signed.杯测分、水分、瑕疵率、农残、交付窗口——全部数字化并签字。

4 · First-purchase exclusivity4 · 排他采购

Grade-A output goes to us or our roaster network first.达标豆优先售予平台或指定烘焙商。

5 · Exit mechanism5 · 退出机制

Equipment buyback, seedling disposal, de-badging, customer handover — pre-agreed.设备回购、种苗处理、摘牌、客户交接——事先约定。

6 · Breach & bio-asset risk6 · 违约与生物资产风险

Off-channel sales, leaks, cross-territory dumping — priced. Power loss & pump failure — insured, with 48-hour root-zone rules.私售、泄密、窜货——明码定价。断电断泵——保险覆盖，执行 48 小时根域规则。
